    "Use `TEM3.EventManager()` to obtain the event management object.\n",

    "|---|---|\n",
    "| `datasize` | Size of data stored in the queue |\n",
    "| `maxsize` | Maximum number of events the queue can hold |\n",
    "| `enabled` | Whether the event is enabled |\n",
    "\n",
    "#### `TEM3.EventKind`\n",
